About 3 Core Sensor Cable

Reliable surveillance cables engineered for long runs, low signal loss, and all-weather use. 3 Core Sensor Cable is a trusted choice for installers and engineers who demand reliable signal transmission and long-term durability.

JCI Cables supplies this product from its manufacturing and warehousing facility in Wazirpur, New Delhi. Bulk orders, custom cut lengths, and OEM enquiries are welcome.

  • Optimised for CCTV & HD surveillance systems
  • Tinned copper conductor for corrosion resistance
  • Low-capacitance for sharp video quality
  • Flexible yet durable PVC sheath
  • Easy to terminate with BNC / RCA connectors
  • Ideal for both indoor and outdoor installation

3-Core Cable Construction

A 3-core cable carries three individually insulated copper conductors within a single PVC outer sheath. Used for CCTV, sensors, and control wiring requiring multiple signal or power lines in a single run.
