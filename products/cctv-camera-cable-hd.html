About CCTV Camera Cable (HD)

Reliable surveillance cables engineered for long runs, low signal loss, and all-weather use. CCTV Camera Cable (HD) is a trusted choice for installers and engineers who demand reliable signal transmission and long-term durability.

JCI Cables supplies this product from its manufacturing and warehousing facility in Wazirpur, New Delhi. Bulk orders, custom cut lengths, and OEM enquiries are welcome.

  • Optimised for CCTV & HD surveillance systems
  • Tinned copper conductor for corrosion resistance
  • Low-capacitance for sharp video quality
  • Flexible yet durable PVC sheath
  • Easy to terminate with BNC / RCA connectors
  • Ideal for both indoor and outdoor installation

2-Core CCTV Cable Construction

A 2-core CCTV cable integrates two individually shielded coaxial conductors in a single flat jacket — one for the video signal and one for power. The RG59 construction ensures excellent video quality over long CCTV runs.
